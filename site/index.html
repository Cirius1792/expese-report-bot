Your expenses, extracted.

Snap a receipt. Type a sentence. Get a clean expense log.

(ex)SpenserBot is a self-hosted Telegram bot that turns receipt photos, PDFs, and free text into structured expenses — amount, currency, merchant, date, category — using LLM-powered extraction.

Self-host it View source

Features

Everything between "I spent money" and "here's the CSV for accounting".

📸

Receipt photo extraction

Send a photo of any receipt — paper or digital — and get amount, currency, merchant, date, and category extracted automatically.

📄

PDF receipts

Long invoices? Send a PDF of 1–5 pages. Multi-page documents are treated as a single receipt.

✍️

Free-text logging

No receipt? Just type "lunch 15.50 eur at Mario's Pizzeria" — ideal for cash expenses. The LLM structures it for you.

🔁

Correction loop

If the extraction is missing something, the bot tells you exactly what and asks for it. You can refine up to 3 times before it gives up gracefully.

📊

Monthly CSV reports

/report exports the current month as a ready-to-use CSV for accounting, reimbursement, or tax prep.

🗑️

Delete with audit trail

Remove mistakes by command or from the inline delete button. Deletions are tracked, not silently erased.

Use cases

Built for anyone who chases receipts after the fact.

🧳

Travel & business trips

Log every trip expense on the go, export the month, send it to finance for reimbursement.

🏠

Personal budgeting

Browse spending by month and year with /list, see totals at a glance, spot where the money goes.

🧾

Freelancer tax prep

Multi-currency by design — expenses keep their original currency, no forced conversion. One clean CSV at the end of the year.

👥

Small teams

Every user sees only their own expenses. A whitelist controls who can use the bot at all.

How it works

From "spent money" to saved record in four steps.

Send

Message the bot on Telegram with a receipt photo, a PDF (≤5 pages), or a plain-text description.

Extract

An LLM extracts amount, currency, merchant, date, and a suggested category from your input.

Confirm or correct

The bot confirms what it understood — or asks for the missing fields. You reply, it refines (up to 3 rounds).

Report

Browse with /list, export with /report. Your monthly CSV is ready for accounting.

Commands & buttons

Everything is one tap away — type a command or use the button bar below the input field.

Your data stays yours

(ex)SpenserBot is self-hosted — no cloud middleman, no data leaving your server.

🔒

Self-hosted storage

Expenses live in a SQLite database on your machine. You can inspect, back up, or wipe it anytime.

👤

Per-user isolation

Each Telegram user sees and manages only their own expenses. No cross-user leaks, ever.

✅

Whitelist & audit

Only Telegram user IDs on your whitelist can use the bot. Unauthorized attempts are logged, not processed.
